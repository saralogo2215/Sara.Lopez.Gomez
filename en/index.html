Sara López-Gómez

Researcher in socio-environmental communication · PhD in Communication, University of Málaga

My research critically examines how public discourses and imaginaries around socio-environmental issues are constructed, communicated and legitimised, with particular attention to gender, ethnicity, social class and Global North–South relations as mediators of meaning.

I draw on interdisciplinary frameworks spanning media studies, political ecology, ecofeminist theory and Latin American decolonial thought. I have contributed to nationally funded research projects, published in leading peer-reviewed journals and undertaken research stays at universities across Europe and Latin America.
