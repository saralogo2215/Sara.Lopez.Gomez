Comunicación medioambiental y climática: Un enfoque holístico de cobertura mediática, organizaciones, educación, activismo y narrativas para la transición ecosocial

[Environmental and climate communication: A holistic approach to media coverage, organisations, education, activism and narratives for the ecosocial transition]

López-Gómez, S.
Doctoral dissertation, University of Málaga

Abstract

This doctoral dissertation, situated in the field of environmental and climate communication, proposes the notion of communication for the ecosocial transition as an integrative approach that brings together traditionally fragmented areas. The research is structured around five interrelated dimensions: media coverage, educommunication, organisational communication, citizen activism and communication for the ecosocial transition. Through seven publications, it examines diverse cases: the influence of lobbies and think tanks on US digital press coverage of COP25; the klimat-live climate section of the Swedish newspaper Aftonbladet; popular educommunication experiences in defence of the Colombian Amazon; the relationship between access to information, activism and climate literacy among Spanish university students; corporate communication through environmental branded podcasts; academic research on activism and climate change; and the role of English- and Spanish-language ecosocial podcasts as spaces for civic imagination and cultural transformation. The results show that the boundaries between these areas are increasingly blurred. Media communication informs, raises visibility and frames problems; educommunication turns knowledge into collective learning; organisational communication legitimises action and contributes to social responsibility and accountability; activism mobilises and reinterprets discourses; and communication for the ecosocial transition integrates and re-signifies these practices towards structural change. The dissertation concludes that the field should be approached from a more holistic and interdisciplinary perspective, moving beyond a merely informative emphasis on climate change, and developed from a transition perspective in dialogue with political ecology, education, social movements and critical economics, towards transformative communication committed to the sustainability of life and to processes of cultural, political and civilisational change.
