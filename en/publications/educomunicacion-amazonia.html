Educomunicación para la preservación de la Amazonía. Estudio de caso Grupo Comunicarte, Colombia

[Educommunication for the Preservation of the Amazon: A Case Study of Grupo Comunicarte, Colombia]

López-Gómez, S.
Revista Española de Desarrollo y Cooperación, 51(2)

Abstract

This article analyses the crucial role of educommunication in protecting the Amazon rainforest and the Indigenous cultures of Colombia, highlighting the environmental, socio-economic and humanitarian challenges the region faces as a result of deforestation, illegal mining and armed conflict. It underlines the structural inequality experienced by ethnic communities, including Afro-descendant and Indigenous populations, and stresses the importance of community initiatives to counter these threats. It also emphasises the need for media representation that reflects the cultural diversity of these communities. It concludes by calling for educational and communication strategies that promote the preservation of Indigenous languages and cultures by valuing the cosmogonies and knowledge of native communities, as well as for more inclusive and diverse media representation in Colombia.
