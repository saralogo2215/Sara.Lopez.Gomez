Branded podcasts medioambientales: Desafíos y oportunidades ante la crisis ecosocial

[Environmental Branded Podcasts: Challenges and Opportunities in the Face of the Ecosocial Crisis]

López-Gómez, S., & Pérez-Ordóñez, C.
Revista Internacional de Relaciones Públicas, 14(28)

Abstract

This study examines environmental branded podcasts as a strategic communication tool for social responsibility (SR), in response to growing concern about the ecosocial crisis. These podcasts allow organisations to align themselves with concerns about socio-environmental challenges and to build a more authentic connection with their publics. This exploratory and descriptive research combines an in-depth interview with a mixed-methods content analysis of 88 Spanish-language podcasts hosted on Spotify. Results show that companies, NGOs and universities are the main producers of this content, with a significant increase between 2020 and 2023. Spain, Colombia and Mexico stand out as the countries with the most environmental branded podcasts. The main topics include the climate crisis, ecological resource management and renewable solutions. There is a correlation between the type of creator organisation and recent podcast activity: companies and universities tend to keep their branded podcasts more active. Podcasts referenced on the organisation’s website are also more likely to remain active, suggesting that when integrated into overall organisational communication strategies they show greater continuity. Podcasts offer advantages such as strengthening reputation and building engaged communities while requiring fewer technical resources. However, they face challenges such as difficulty reaching large audiences due to their niche focus, the risk of being perceived as greenwashing, and the possible growing use of artificial intelligence in their production.
