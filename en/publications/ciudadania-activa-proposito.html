Ciudadanía activa y comunicación con propósito

[Active Citizenship and Purpose-Driven Communication]

Barroso, M.-B., López-Gómez, S., Castro-Martínez, A., & Castillo-Esparcia, A.
In: A. Álvarez-Nobell, I. Ruiz-Mora & M.-B. Barroso (Eds.), Repensar la confianza: Organizaciones con propósito, ciudadanías y comunicación (pp. 89–111). Tirant Humanidades

Abstract

Knowing, wanting and doing are three essentials in tackling the climate emergency. This chapter starts from the idea that purpose—understood as an organisation’s reason for being, rooted in knowledge, beliefs and values—crystallises into behaviour, and asks what role companies and their communication play in transforming the economic and social model. It explores three key concepts: active citizenship, purpose-driven companies and purpose-driven communication. It examines how the excess of greenwashing and other forms of “washing” limits access to the information people need in order to act, and how purpose-driven organisations can become catalysts for citizen activism. The chapter traces the shift from an informational model to genuine dialogue with publics, communicative innovation and the evaluation of positive and expected impacts, and proposes a basic template for designing a purpose-driven communication plan.
