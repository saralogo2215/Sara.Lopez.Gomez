Comercio justo e igualdad de género. Estudio de caso en Ecuador, Perú y México

[Fair Trade and Gender Equality. Case Study in Ecuador, Peru and Mexico]

Quesada Herrera, M. E., Jan Argüello, M., Angulo Garófalo, S., & López-Gómez, S.
Medicus Mundi International and Basque Agency for Development Cooperation

Abstract

Research carried out by a multidisciplinary team of academics, fair trade organisations and public institutions from Latin America and Spain, together with medicusmundi Araba, on the progress in gender equality achieved through fair trade initiatives. The study was funded by the Basque Agency for Development Cooperation.
